SMART PORTER – Entity Relationship Diagram (Textual)

================================================================================
ENTITIES & RELATIONSHIPS
================================================================================

users (user_id PK)
  | 1:1
  +-- passengers (passenger_id PK, user_id FK UNIQUE)
  +-- porters (porter_id PK, user_id FK UNIQUE)
  +-- admin (admin_id PK, user_id FK UNIQUE)

stations (station_id PK)
  | 1:N
  +-- station_locations (location_id PK, station_id FK)
  +-- porters.current_station_id (FK, nullable)
  +-- porter_availability (station_id FK)
  +-- bookings.station_id (FK)

service_types (service_type_id PK)
  | 1:N
  +-- bookings.service_type_id (FK)

bookings (booking_id PK, booking_code UNIQUE)
  | N:1 passengers, porters, stations, station_locations (pickup/drop), service_types
  | 1:1
  +-- luggage_details
  +-- fare_details
  +-- payments
  +-- feedback
  | 1:N
  +-- booking_status_history

porters
  | 1:1
  +-- porter_availability

notifications (user_id FK)
fare_settings (standalone config)

================================================================================
CARDINALITY SUMMARY
================================================================================
users           1 ---- 1   passengers / porters / admin
passengers      1 ---- N   bookings
porters         1 ---- N   bookings
stations        1 ---- N   station_locations, bookings
service_types   1 ---- N   bookings
bookings        1 ---- 1   luggage_details, fare_details, payments, feedback
bookings        1 ---- N   booking_status_history

Normalization: 3NF (no transitive dependencies, atomic attributes, full functional dependency on PK).
